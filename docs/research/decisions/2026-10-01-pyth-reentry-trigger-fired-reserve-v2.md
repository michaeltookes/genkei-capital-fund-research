## Phase B — counter-thesis and the reflection on 9/17

**Strongest case against the add:** this is regret-driven chasing after a +39% rally. September purchases may reflect a treasury-balance event rather than durable growth, and V2 does not commit future monthly revenue to buying. Customer collections and post-paywall churn remain unverified. **What I am most likely overweighting:** the purchase rebound and the desk's recent missed rallies. **Mitigation:** label the initial add discretionary, require both revenue-funded purchases and disclosed conversion/churn before the second discipline tranche, and retain the price and calendar exits. **Reflection on the 2026-09-17 call (recorded here because that file is being superseded):** its central mechanism claim — "the revenue → Reserve → buyback flywheel is not visibly spinning on-chain" — was **wrong within fourteen days**, and it was wrong for a reason the file itself identified and then dismissed in its own Phase B: the buyback series under the one-third rule measured *treasury balance*, not *flow*, and the Douro Labs reports the file cited were already disclosing ~$170K/month of remittances.
