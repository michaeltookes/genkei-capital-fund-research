# Crypto broad-market accumulation — BTC trigger fired

## Frame

This file exists to close the trigger-fire lifecycle from `2026-07-19-crypto-bottom-top50-accumulation-thesis`. That decision said broad accumulation should begin when aggregate stablecoin supply turned net-positive for 2+ weeks **OR** BTC decisively reclaimed >$72k. The 2026-09-02 Robinhood Chain session recorded BTC around $78-79k, so the price leg has fired even though the stablecoin-flow lake remains dark.

## Phase A — case for and case against

**Case for broadening now:** the July decision deliberately made BTC price a sufficient trigger so the desk would not wait indefinitely for flow confirmation after a real upside break. A move from the $63-64k base to ~$78-79k is not a marginal tick through resistance; it is a decisive reclaim. The trigger was a forward commitment, and honoring it preserves the value of the decision log.

**Case for caution:** the same September read still shows no clean liquidity confirmation: stablecoin flow is unverifiable after Aug 1 and the last lake print had Ethereum stablecoins bleeding, while BTC ETF flows were negative YTD. That argues against indiscriminate max-size top-50 buying and in favor of staged additions, but it does not reverse the OR condition that already fired.

## Phase B — counter-thesis

The strongest objection is that broadening on price alone buys a bear-market squeeze without the dry-powder signal the July file called decisive. That risk is real, which is why the action here is staged broadening, not a one-shot basket buy. The rebuttal is process discipline: the July file explicitly accepted BTC >$72k as an independent confirmation path, and weakening that condition after it fires would turn the trigger into a suggestion. The live risk control is sizing, not pretending the trigger remains pending.

## Conclusion

**Recommendation: broaden from selective BTC/SOL scaling to staged active accumulation across the approved crypto watchlist / higher-quality top-50 set.** Do not wait for stablecoin-flow confirmation to start broadening; use the repaired flow data to decide pace and size. Keep weak-fundamental tail names governed by their own asset-level decisions — SUI-like sell/avoid calls do not become buys merely because the macro gate fired. Confidence is **medium**: the price trigger is clean, but flow confirmation remains missing while the lake is dark.
