# SUI — Raoul Pal's "AI agents will choose Sui" thesis, evidence-tested

## Frame

Michael asks whether Raoul Pal's thesis — AI agents will pick Sui as the payments chain, on zero gas fees for stablecoin transfers plus architecture/speed/finality — has merit, or whether Pal is "paid a large bag from the Sui Foundation and will just shill the project no matter what." He recalls the desk previously favoring Solana for this role (no logged decision file makes that call — it was conversational; this file is the first logged position on agentic-payment chains). Stakes: SUI is the tactical sleeve's exit-underway position (2026-08-05 file); a validated Pal thesis would argue for halting the exit or re-entering. **What would change the answer:** measurable agent-payment flow actually routing to Sui, or sustained growth in the chain's stablecoin float. Float growth is one possible adoption signal; higher payment velocity can also use an unchanged float.

## Macro context

Regime is risk_on (4/4 inputs, per the 09-20 ZEC session — unchanged) but 2026 is a rotation tape: BTC/ETH/SOL negative YTD with leadership concentrated in ZEC (+181%) and HYPE (~+260%). SUI has NOT participated in the rotation: ~$1.01, **−28% YTD, −81% from its Jan-2025 ATH**, and −22.9% relative to SOL even over the recovery 90 days (lake RS). A narrative this strong (Pal has been pushing it since 2024's "the chosen one") that produces no relative bid in a rotation-hungry market is itself evidence the market doesn't believe it yet.

## Fundamentals — the claims vs the chain

**What's REAL in Pal's pitch (verified):**
- Protocol-level **zero-gas P2P stablecoin transfers** activated 2026-05-20 for 7 stablecoins (USDC, USDsui, suiUSDe, USDY, FDUSD, AUSD, USDB) via the Address Balances mechanism; users need not hold SUI; Fireblocks integrated. This is more than app-level gas sponsorship — it is a genuine, currently-unique protocol feature.
- **Mysticeti consensus ~390–400ms finality** on mainnet; programmable transaction blocks (up to 1,024 actions atomically — a genuinely good primitive for multi-step agent workflows); zkLogin; native USDC since Oct 2024.

**What's WRONG or overstated:**
- Zero-gas covers **simple P2P stablecoin transfers only** — swaps, contract calls, and any real agent workflow still pay gas (avg ~$0.0005; Sui's own blog says "<$0.02"). Solana's equivalent cost is <$0.01 — the "zero vs sub-cent" gap is economically irrelevant to any agent transacting for a purpose. Who absorbs Sui's zero-gas cost (and its anti-abuse limits) is undocumented — an unpriced subsidy, not physics.
- Headline volume needs attribution: [the June 14 report citing CertiK](https://news.bitcoin.com/sui-blockchain-registers-65-billion-in-stablecoin-volume-following-major-fee-removal-protocol/) records **$65B in stablecoin transfers since June 10**. Using five calendar days and a $490M float gives **~27x supply turnover per day** as an illustrative estimate, not a measured daily series. [The follow-up report](https://news.bitcoin.com/crypto-news/sui-stablecoin-supply-500-million-zero-fee-transfers/) notes that CertiK supplied no stablecoin or counterparty breakdown. Bot loops are a hypothesis; high velocity is also compatible with payments. Address-level attribution and adjusted flows are needed to distinguish economic payments, agent activity and repeated transfers; the aggregate alone establishes none of those shares. The "6M TPS" figure was a marketing stunt ~20x Sui's lab ceiling; real-time throughput runs ~107 TPS (max recorded ~1,204).
- Alpenglow is a potential future challenge to Sui's mainnet finality advantage. [Solana's September 28 update](https://solana.com/podcasts/solana-weekly-news/episodes/solana-finality-is-getting-85x-faster-e3pgsst) reports **testnet deployment targeting 150ms finality**, not mainnet activation. That target would be faster than Mysticeti if delivered on mainnet; the cited update supplies no mainnet activation date, so it cannot establish that Sui's current latency advantage has expired or will expire this month/next.

- **Reliability matters for the zero-gas thesis:** [Sui's May 31 postmortem](https://www.sui.io/blog/sui-mainnet-halts-resolved-after-major-upgrade) records three mainnet outages on May 28–29 after the Address Balances rollout. The first two arose from interactions between address balances and gas charging; the third involved randomness state lost across validator restarts. The postmortem reports the known bugs fixed, no funds at risk and no committed transactions reverted, but also identifies missing containment for inputs that crash validators. This is direct reliability evidence for the payment primitive: throughput and finality alone do not establish readiness for production agent payments.

**The evidence test (September 2026 observations; evidence qualifications corrected at PR review on October 6):**
- **x402** (the only agentic-payment standard with published on-chain volume): Coinbase's facilitator supports Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, World, **Solana — not Sui**. For Aug 22–Sept 19, [CoinNess's September 22 report citing Artemis data](https://coinness.com/en/news/1169480) gives **Solana 76% (23.2M transactions), XRP Ledger 3.39M, Polygon PoS 2.05M, and Base 1.85M**. Both XRP Ledger and Polygon exceed Base; Sui is absent from this reported breakdown. These are reported transaction counts, not a complete census of agentic payments or proof of zero Sui activity. (Absolute dollars remain small — ~$3.3M/week — the standard is young; the SHARE is the signal.)
- **Google AP2**: Mysten/Sui named only in secondary coverage of the A2A x402 extension (unconfirmed on Google's own materials); Solana has that plus a first-party Google Cloud "Pay.sh" stablecoin-AI launch.
- **Card-network programs**: [Visa's nine-chain announcement](https://investor.visa.com/news/news-details/2026/Visa-Accelerates-Stablecoin-Momentum-Adding-Five-Blockchains-for-Settlement/) concerns its **issuer/acquirer stablecoin-settlement pilot**, not a Visa Intelligent Commerce chain-support list. [Mastercard's Agent Pay for Machines announcement](https://investor.mastercard.com/investor-news/investor-news-details/2026/Mastercard-Launches-Agent-Pay-for-Machines-to-Unlock-Super-Fast-Always-On-Payments/default.aspx) describes settlement across cards, accounts and stablecoins and lists participants/supporters, not supported blockchains. Sui is not named in these materials, but that absence does **not** establish that either agentic program excludes Sui or routes payments around it. Agentic-payment chain support remains unverified from these sources.
- **The float**: Sui stablecoin supply **$490M — flat four straight months** (lake: $482M Jun 22 → $457M → $490M → $490M Sept 21) vs Solana $16.1B (33x) and Base ~$5B. Sui doesn't crack the lake's top-20 chains. This shows no sustained expansion of resident stablecoin capital in these snapshots. It does not rule out payments or agent activity using the existing float at higher velocity.
- Sui's own Sept 2026 agentic-commerce post names zero commercial partners and zero production agent-payment volume — primitives and demos.

## Flow & positioning

- The desk's SUI ETF snapshot is SUIS/GSUI/TSUI combined ≈ **$50M AUM**. AUM is not cumulative net inflows. ZCSH's [SEC registration](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1720265/000119312526362505/zcsh_8-a12b.htm) records the August 24 renaming of the existing Grayscale Zcash Trust ahead of exchange listing. [The September 18 report](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-09-18-grayscales-zcash-etf-plans-3-for-1-split-233-million-inflow-surge-415520) distinguishes roughly **$890M in net assets as of September 17** from **more than $233M of inflows since August 25**. Total assets include the pre-conversion holdings and valuation changes as well as new flows; the cited figures do not quantify each component. Without comparable SUI net creations over the same window, these AUM snapshots do not establish the claimed investor-preference gap.
- Unlock overhang persists: ~41% circulating of 10B; ~60% of supply still to emit through ~2030 (FDV $10B vs $4.1B mcap). The B-145 unlock table now covers all 8 allocations for the record, but Sui's is the slow-drip profile, not a cliff story.
- Staking flow (freshly repaired B-088 collector): net pending ~0 — neutral, no institutional stake build.

## Phase A — case for and against Pal's thesis

**For:** the primitives are real and well-designed (zero-gas P2P, PTBs, sub-second finality, zkLogin); Circle is native; Fireblocks integrated; the architecture offers useful capabilities for agent frameworks, subject to demonstrated production reliability after the May outages; conference season (Basecamp, Oct 7–8, "agentic economy" themed) could produce a real partner announcement; the asset is so washed out (−81% from ATH, $4B cap) that thesis validation from here would be violently reflexive.

**Against:** the cited x402 transaction breakdown is led by Solana, followed by XRP Ledger, Polygon and Base, with Sui absent from that report; resident stablecoin capital has not expanded across the four monthly snapshots, while the reported transfer volume remains unattributed; the cost advantage over Solana is ~half a cent; and the loudest advocate has structural incentives (below). The Address Balances outages add a concrete reliability concern even though the known bugs were fixed. Alpenglow is a testnet-stage competitive risk, not a shipped mainnet latency comparison. The Visa/Mastercard announcements do not establish agentic-payment routing by chain and add no evidence of Sui exclusion.

## Phase B — counter-thesis and the Pal-incentive question

**On Pal specifically (the "paid bag" question, factually):** Pal **confirmed on X (Nov 2024) that he sits on the Sui Foundation board** ("I am a mercenary for my own capital"), and **Real Vision has a formal Sui partnership** (June 2025, terms undisclosed). Compensation for the board seat is undisclosed; his SUI position size is undisclosed; and the Sept 2026 Milk Road coverage of his "highest-conviction agentic play" framing carried **no board-seat disclosure**. He also made the *same* best-chain-for-AI-payments argument for **Solana** at Consensus 2026, and his track record on conviction calls includes top-ticking ETH "irresponsibly long" within weeks of the Nov-2021 peak and serially extending "Banana Zone" timelines. Verdict on the man: **not a fabricator — the features he cites are real — but a maximally conflicted narrator** whose thesis should be weighted like an issuer's marketing deck: mine it for testable claims, never for conclusions.

**Strongest counter to this file's skepticism:** x402 is 18 months old and its absolute volumes ($3.3M/week) are so small that the reported Solana-led distribution across Solana, XRP Ledger, Polygon and Base could be pre-paradigm noise — the way "Ethereum has all the developers" looked dispositive in 2020 right before Solana took payments share anyway. Sui's zero-gas primitive plus PTB atomicity could be a useful fit for high-frequency machine flows **if** a first-party integration lands and demonstrates reliable operation; comparative suitability remains unproven. That is a real option value — but it is an option, and options this far out of the money are what the reopen triggers are for. The desk does not need to predict the winner; it needs to detect the winner early, and the lake already carries one useful instrument (`stablecoin-flow`, per-chain), to be assessed alongside attributed payment activity and integration reliability. Float need not move first if adoption increases velocity instead.

## Conclusion

**Pal's thesis remains unvalidated by the cited evidence, and the desk's informal Solana lean is now the logged position.** The technically-real core (zero-gas P2P, 400ms finality, PTBs) is narrower than advertised, and the economic edge over Solana is ~half a cent. Alpenglow's 150ms target is on testnet; it does not yet overturn Sui's mainnet finality advantage, and no mainnet date is established by the cited update. The reported x402 transaction distribution favors Solana (76%), with XRP Ledger, Polygon and Base also represented and Sui absent from that breakdown. This supports a provisional Solana lean, not a claim that all measurable agentic payments route to Solana/Base. Visa's settlement pilot and Mastercard's participant roster do not establish their agentic programs' chain support and are excluded from that inference. The lake records **Sui's stablecoin float flat at ~$490M across four monthly snapshots** with the zero-gas feature live throughout. That does not establish an absence of payments: the reported $65B transfer volume remains a usage signal whose agent/payment share is unknown. The May Address Balances outages also prevent treating architecture as a settled strength for production payments. The avoid call rests on unvalidated agent-specific adoption and reliability, not a proven bot-only explanation for volume or an ETF AUM-based preference ranking. Pal is not inventing features, but he is a Sui Foundation board member with a Real Vision–Sui partnership and inconsistent disclosure, who has made the same argument for Solana — treat his conviction as marketing, not evidence. **Action: avoid** — the 2026-08-05 exit stands; do not halt it or re-enter on this narrative. The sleeve's expression of the agentic-payments theme remains SOL (crypto-core, already held), which leads the cited x402 transaction counts. Reopen only on the trigger's evidence gates — stablecoin float 3x-ing to $1.5B+, Sui registering in x402 share data, or a first-party integration naming Sui — not on Basecamp announcements or further advocacy.
