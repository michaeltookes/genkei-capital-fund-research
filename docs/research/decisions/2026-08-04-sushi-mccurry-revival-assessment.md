**Token accrual:** SUSHI actually has one of DeFi's older real mechanisms — xSUSHI staking receives a share of eligible swap fees. But the current combined protocol mix does not pass the whole ~$7–10M/yr gross-fee base through the legacy 0.05%/0.30% split: DeFiLlama's holder-revenue view shows 2026 token-holder net income of only ~$0.31M YTD (Q1 $187K + Q2 $110K + Q3-to-date $15K), roughly ~$0.6M annualized, with the 30d holder-revenue run rate closer to ~$0.4M/yr. Against a $45.7M market cap, that is roughly **75–110× actual holder revenue** — and attached to a business whose top line has declined every year but one since 2021. The mechanism exists; the measured holder cash flow is not yet large enough to make token accrual an investable offset to the usage decline.

**The shipping is real — Michael's perception is confirmed:** Sushi Labs is genuinely building — Blade (IL-free AMM), Wara (Solana-native trading), Susa (on-chain order-book perps on N1), Kubo (delta-neutral perps), Sushi Launch on Robinhood Chain, SushiXSwap expansion. New CEO Alex McCurry arrived Dec 2025 with a Synthesis strategic investment (10M+ SUSHI acquired, ~$2–3M at the time). **But look at what the new products attack:** Wara enters Solana trading against Jupiter's dominance; Susa/Kubo enter perps against Hyperliquid, while Sushi Launch is a live launchpad/RWA-adjacent path that would matter only if it shows up in Sushi V3 or token-holder revenue. The desk holds JUP, while HYPE is a staged/inactive fill-gated decision rather than an owned position. The revival strategy is to assault the two most entrenched franchises in DeFi with ~1/100th their traction, plus a live launchpad channel that still needs measured revenue proof, from a $46M market cap. Shipping ≠ usage: after 8+ months of the new-products roadmap, TVL is still down ~23% from the Dec 2025 transition baseline and 2026 fees are pacing as the worst year in the series.

**Provenance (§5b, promoter = the CEO):** McCurry is verified — Solidity.io founder, Forbes 30-Under-30 (2023), took over Dec 2025. ## Phase A — case for and case against

**Bull case:**
1. New, motivated, well-marketed leadership with capital and personal token exposure; at $46M mcap, any success re-rates violently.
2. Real token accrual plumbing (xSUSHI fee share) already exists — no tokenomics rebuild needed if eligible holder revenue ever scales again.
3. Fully diluted — no unlock overhang, unlike most of the sector.
4. Brand recognition survives from the 2020–21 era; multichain deployment (40+ chains) is distribution most $46M projects lack.
5. Optionality math: if any one new product or launch channel works, the base is so low that 5–10× is plausible.

**Bear case:**
1. **Multi-year usage decline, with only a 2024 interruption, and still declining**: fees −98.7% from 2021, TVL −76% from the trailing-year peak and still ~23% below the Dec 2025 transition baseline. The single most thesis-relevant series says the turnaround is not happening yet.
2. **The strategy attacks entrenched incumbents the desk already owns or has staged**: Wara vs Jupiter, Susa/Kubo vs Hyperliquid. Base rate for #5 players re-entering categories against dominant network-effect leaders is dismal, and the desk already has the cleaner incumbent expression in JUP plus a fill-gated HYPE plan.
3. **DEX spot is a lost war**: Uniswap + chain-native DEXes + aggregators took the market years ago; Sushi's share is a rounding error and its core product has no differentiated reason to win it back.
4. **~75–110× measured holder revenue is not cheap** — the LQTY lesson verbatim: "no one is looking at it" is true because the protocol is in decline, not because value is hidden.
5. **Promoter-sourced discovery**: the channel exists to build the community around the token its owner holds. That is the LQTY/Mahoney pattern executed at CEO grade — better production values, same information content: zero until the data confirms.

## Phase B — counter-thesis

**Strongest case for being wrong:** turnarounds look exactly like this at the bottom — new operator, capital injection, product blitz, price at maximum pessimism — and McCurry is a legitimately capable operator, not a grifter; 8 months is genuinely too short for Blade/Wara/Susa/Kubo/Sushi Launch to show in fees, so "no inflection yet" may simply be "too early to measure," and by the time the fee series confirms, the first 3–5× from $46M is gone. This is real, and it's why the reassessment trigger is *usage-based and generous* (2 quarters of fee growth + $150M TVL, Blade/Wara/Susa/Kubo reaching top-10 traction with holder revenue, or Sushi Launch adding sustained Sushi V3/holder revenue) rather than price-based: if the revival is real, the data will show it with most of the upside still intact — a $46M-cap turnaround that works doesn't stop at 2×. What the counter-thesis cannot explain away: the *existing* core business (the thing generating today's fees) has no visible path to reversal against Uniswap and chain-native venues, so the bet reduces largely to new-product lottery tickets in categories owned by JUP and HYPE, plus the still-unproven Robinhood Chain launchpad channel — and the desk can already express conviction in the DEX/perps categories by holding JUP and staging HYPE at a fill-gated entry rather than funding the challenger. **Calibration note:** this is the desk's second promoter-surfaced avoid in ten days (LQTY 2026-07-24, confidence high, same shape); the discipline that call encoded — let the lake/data decide, log the provenance — applies with a stronger fee series and a more credible promoter, which changes the tone but not the arithmetic.

## Conclusion

**Recommendation: AVOID — no position; the write-off is earned on today's data, but it's logged with a real reopen path.** Michael's instinct was right in direction and wrong in comparison: Sushi is not JUP-shaped — Jupiter is a *dominant* aggregator with ~100× Sushi's fee scale and a 50% fee buyback; Sushi is what a DEX token looks like *after* losing its market, which is precisely why the analogy flatters it. The shipping is real, the CEO is real and capable, the token accrual mechanism exists — and neither usage nor measured holder revenue has bent enough to matter while TVL remains ~23% below the Dec 2025 transition baseline. Sleeve: crypto-tactical (would-be), horizon months, confidence **high** — matching the LQTY calibration: when the direct usage evidence is unambiguous and multi-year, the desk's high-confidence skeptical calls have been its best. Top risks to the avoid: (1) too-early-to-measure — covered by the usage triggers, which reopen with most upside intact; (2) a pure narrative/beta squeeze re-rates SUSHI without fundamentals (16%-of-mcap daily volume makes it squeeze-capable) — accepted; the desk doesn't chase squeezes; (3) McCurry's marketing engine succeeds in rebuilding the community into genuine usage and xSUSHI/token-holder income — that *is* the trigger condition, so it's captured, not dismissed. Watch his channel for inspiration; keep the capital in JUP and the fill-gated HYPE plan unless Sushi's usage and holder-revenue data turn.
